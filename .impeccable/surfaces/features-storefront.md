# Storefront (customer) surface brief

Scope: every customer route (home, services, service detail, team, provider, providers, gallery, reviews, gift cards, contact, book, booking confirmation, appointments, account, sign in and sign up, intake, join) and the coming-soon and not-found states. Mode: Persuade on home; Operate on booking and account. Audience and job are in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: every route, state and workflow stays; no clinical claims; the owner pinned a dark, premium aesthetic (2026-10-24 brief).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The storefront is a stack of charcoal-dyed spa linen. The page is the darkest fold, and every band and card is a fold a tone lighter whose top edge catches the light. Colour pages are linen dyed in the practice's hue, and the treatment menu reads like a printed annual hung on one axis. It refuses the bone-paper dewdrop page (the previous look) and the blush-pink spa template.
OWN-WORLD: Charcoal linen ground (oklch 16.5%, warm), folds at 20.5% and 24.5% with a 1px light crease, warm off-white ink, and the accent from the practice's logo hue for the primary action and dyed bands. EB Garamond (vendored, OFL) sets everything, with italic for side notes. Soft corners at 4, 6 and 8px; no outlines, no glow, no blur.
STORY: The client sees the practice's name, whether it's open, today's hours and how to call, picks a concern or treatment, chooses a provider, books a slot, and completes intake.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The hero sits on a 42/58 axis. Left: side notes (locality, open status, the treatments, today and call facts as folded bands) and the portrait capsule. Right: the practice name up to 136px, treatment-category chips, and Book an appointment with View treatments. On a phone the name and booking come first.
FORM: Folded spa linen, number 3 on my ordered list (1 vanity mirror ring light, 2 apothecary dropper labels, 3 folded spa linen, 4 injector's face-mapping chart, 5 appointment card, 6 hammam steam room, 7 powder compact). seed:27e1958b2fe14c8b. Raises: everything registers against one off-centre axis (calibrated working plate); colour comes as whole flat fields, never a wash (solidarity silkscreen). Signature interaction: folds open once as they arrive (clip from the top), and each fold darkens to the next tone when pressed.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
